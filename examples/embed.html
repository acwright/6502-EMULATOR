Embedding the 6502 emulator

Reference: docs/EMBEDDING.md. Serving from — override with ?base=….

1. Two embeds on one page

Click into one and type: only the focused frame takes the keyboard, and it shows a focus ring while it does. Tab between them, or click the page background to give the keyboard back to this document — arrow keys and space should scroll here, not disappear into an emulator.

They also show the two shapes of the start prompt. A autostarts, so it is already booting and typing behind a small corner badge; B waits, so it is blank behind a full Click to start and types only once clicked.

A — controls=full, autotyped program, 1 MHz
B — autostart=0, muted=0, 2 MHz

2. Driving a frame over postMessage

Every button below targets embed A. The log shows what comes back: 6502:ready once, then 6502:serial and 6502:stopped as they happen.

3. Loading a program, two ways

The same .prg file, sent as a 6502:load message (no reload) or baked into a fresh frame as prg64= (no network fetch at all — this is the form a docs site wants for inline samples).
