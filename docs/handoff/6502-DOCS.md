# What 6502-DOCS wants from emulator 3.0

Emulator 3.0.0 has two video cards: the **TMS9918A** of 2.x, still the default, and
the **6502-PICOVDP** of [../VDP-SPEC.md](../VDP-SPEC.md), picked with `--vdp picovdp`
or `vdp=picovdp`. [6502-DOCS](https://github.com/acwright/6502-DOCS) proves its
samples and takes its screenshots with the emulator, and embeds live machines in
its pages, so this is the list of what the PICOVDP means there and what it could
add. It was written from the emulator side; 6502-DOCS's own plan takes it up, and
this file is deleted once that plan has absorbed it.

Line numbers are as of 6502-DOCS `85d6bb7`. Section numbers (§) are VDP-SPEC's.
[../MIGRATING.md](../MIGRATING.md) is the emulator-side account of moving a program
to the PICOVDP card.

---

## 0. When this happens — resolved

The site documents the **hardware**, and a real ACE today has a Pico9918 running as
a TMS9918A. The order is settled: the firmware is proven on a PICO9918 PRO v2.0,
BIOS 2.0 is released, and the documentation is rewritten for the new card, with
Graphics II, Multicolor and the F18A section coming out of the rewritten pages
(the PICOVDP does not implement the F18A, §1 non-goals).

What has changed is that nothing waits for the emulator any more. 3.0.0 is on the
emulator's `main`, and the choice of card is a parameter, so the rewrite can move
page by page.

## 1. The embedded machines — resolved

- **`/v2/` is live.** `https://acwright.github.io/6502-EMULATOR/v2/` is release
  2.7.0 (BIOS 1.6), frozen, and 6502-DOCS `v1` embeds it.
- **`main`'s unpinned embeds keep working** after the emulator's merge:
  `embed.html` with no `vdp=` boots the TMS9918A with BIOS 1.6, so the
  `assembly/graphics-2` and `assembly/multicolor` machines draw what they drew.
- **Pages rewritten for the new card pin `vdp=picovdp`** in their embed URLs
  (`data/emulator.json`'s `web.frame` plus the parameter, or `data-6502-vdp` through
  `embed.js`). Legacy-card pages need no parameter until the emulator flips its
  default in a later release; from then they need `vdp=tms9918a`. The emulator
  announces the flip one release ahead.

## 4. Must — screenshots

Captured on `--vdp tms9918a` (the default in 3.0), all eleven PNGs in
`docs/public/images/screens/` are what 2.x produced. Captured on `--vdp picovdp`,
every colour but black and white moves slightly: that card's palette is 12-bit, so
the TMS9918A's sixteen colours are quantized to 4 bits a channel. No channel moves
by more than 8. The emulator's own goldens measured it: the BIOS's black-on-white
screens do not change at all, and Wizards Lab's colours do.

- `graphics-2.png` and `multicolor.png` become pictures of garbage if re-captured
  on the PICOVDP (`scripts/capture-screens.mjs:139-152`)
- `graphics-1.png`'s subject text (130–138) and the `colors` note (96) mention
  Multicolor and the palette
- `screens:verify` exists but is not in `npm run verify` or CI, and `README.md:239-241`
  says the screenshots are not drift-checked while 222–223 and 485 say they are
- `run --headless --console video [--vdp picovdp] --screenshot <file>` now does in one process what
  `capture-screens.mjs` does with a server and `dbg screen png` — optional, but
  simpler for the shots that need no typing

## 8. Must — flash cartridges, from 3.4.0

Emulator 3.4.0 runs the AC6502 Flash Cart. Two things in it reach the
documentation, and one of them is an accuracy divergence that has to be written
down rather than discovered.

**The CLI reference gains two flags**, and `--cart` gains four sizes:

```
--cart <file>             Load a cartridge (32K ROM, or 128K/256K/512K/1M flash)
--cart-save <file>        Save overlay for a flash cart (default: <cart>.sav)
--no-cart-save            Discard flash writes on exit
```

The rules behind them are 6502-VCS `PLAN.md` §1 and §4, which is the normative
copy for the whole rollout and where the *Bigger cartridges* chapter should take
its table from:

- the size picks the mapper, the name never does, and a name that disagrees with
  the byte count is a warning and not an error
- **no emulator ever writes a `.crt`** — not on save, not on eject, not on quit.
  A cartridge that programs its own flash writes a sidecar `.sav` beside the
  image, carrying that image's size and CRC-32 so that rebuilding the cart makes
  the save refuse to apply rather than landing over new code

**`ACCURACY.md` gains two entries**, both agreed across the rollout rather than
particular to this emulator:

- **Open bus reads `$FF`.** Selecting the absent U2 of a one-chip cart reads
  `$FF` for the whole 8 KB window. A simplification, in all three emulators.
- **The busy window is modelled here and not in the other two.** A byte program
  takes 20 µs, a sector erase 25 ms and a chip erase 100 ms, counted in CPU
  cycles, and while the chip is busy every read of it returns status. That is
  what makes a self-programming routine running from flash rather than from RAM
  hang in this emulator exactly as it would on the board — which is the point,
  and worth a sentence in the chapter so that nobody "fixes" it.

[6502-VCS.md](6502-VCS.md) is the full account of what this emulator guarantees
to the hardware, and `samples/flash-cart/` is a worked cartridge with a README.

## 9. Could — new material
