# What 6502-DOCS wants from emulator 3.0

The emulator's video card is now the 6502-PICOVDP of [../VDP-SPEC.md](../VDP-SPEC.md)
rather than a TMS9918A. [6502-DOCS](https://github.com/acwright/6502-DOCS) proves
its samples and takes its screenshots with the emulator, and embeds live machines
in its pages, so this is the list of what that change breaks there and what it
could add. It was written from the emulator side; nothing has been started in that
repository.

Line numbers are as of 6502-DOCS `85d6bb7`. Section numbers (§) are VDP-SPEC's.
[../MIGRATING.md](../MIGRATING.md) is the emulator-side account of every
behaviour that changed.

---

## 0. A decision first

The site documents the **hardware**. Its pages say the emulator runs what the ACE
runs, and until 2.6.9 that was true. A real ACE today has a Pico9918 running as a
TMS9918A; the PICOVDP is replacement firmware that has not been written. So there
are two consistent ways forward, and most of this list depends on which:

1. **The hardware is going to move.** Then the video chapters are rewritten for the
   new card, Graphics II and Multicolor come out, and the F18A section goes —
   the PICOVDP does not implement the F18A (§1, non-goals).
2. **The hardware stays a TMS9918A for now.** Then the video chapters stay as they
   are, and the site needs to say where the emulator and the board part company
   (see [MIGRATING.md](../MIGRATING.md#the-emulator-and-the-board)) — and it can no
   longer prove or photograph the Graphics II, Multicolor and F18A samples with the
   emulator at all.

Either way, §1 below comes before the emulator merges to `main`.

---

## 1. Blocking — the embedded machines are unpinned

`data/emulator.json:22` points every `<Emulator>` on the site at
`https://acwright.github.io/6502-EMULATOR/embed.html`, which is whatever the
emulator's `main` branch last deployed. The emulator's Pages workflow deploys on
every push to `main`. **The moment 3.0.0 lands there**, with no change to the docs:

- the `assembly/graphics-2` and `assembly/multicolor` machines on
  `docs/assembly/graphics.md` (lines 100–103, 128–131) draw garbage
- anything else embedded keeps working — Text and Graphics I are unchanged

Options, from either side:

- remove or replace those two embeds before the emulator merges
- have the emulator deploy a frozen 2.6.9 build under a versioned path
  (e.g. `/6502-EMULATOR/2/embed.html`) and point `data/emulator.json` at it —
  an emulator-side change, not yet made

## 2. Must — the version pin and the harness

Moving the site to 3.0.0 is one edit that fails everything until the rest is done,
by design (`data/emulator.json:15-18`):

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `data/emulator.json:18` | `"version": "2.6.9"` — read by `scripts/preflight.mjs:44-46, 85-111` |
| `.github/workflows/verify.yml:17` | `EMULATOR_REF: v2.6.9` — CI builds the CLI from the tag |
| `scripts/check-voice.mjs:109-118` | fails on any three-part version in `docs/` that isn't the pin |
| `docs/crossdev/debugging.md:56` | a `dbg info` transcript reading `headless 2.6.9` |
| `docs/crossdev/tools.md:26` | a `6502 --version` transcript |
| `README.md:12-13`, `ACCURACY.md:25` | "produced by emulator 2.6.9", the accuracy baseline |

**The harness itself needs no change for 3.0.** `scripts/verify-samples.mjs` and
`scripts/capture-screens.mjs` drive `run --headless --debug`, `dbg wait`,
`dbg screen text`, `dbg state save/load` and `dbg screen png`, all of which still
behave. Snapshots are made fresh each run (`verify-samples.mjs:286, 547-548`) and
never committed, so the emulator refusing 2.x snapshots does not reach it. Boot
stays Text at 40 × 24, so matching `^OK\s*$` on the grid keeps working.

## 4. Must — screenshots

All eleven PNGs in `docs/public/images/screens/` are colour, and every colour but
black and white moves slightly: the card's palette is 12-bit, so the TMS9918A's
sixteen colours are quantized to 4 bits a channel. No channel moves by more than 8.
The emulator's own goldens measured it: the BIOS's black-on-white screens do not
change at all, and Wizards Lab's colours do.

- `graphics-2.png` and `multicolor.png` become pictures of garbage if re-captured
  (`scripts/capture-screens.mjs:139-152`)
- `graphics-1.png`'s subject text (130–138) and the `colors` note (96) mention
  Multicolor and the palette
- `screens:verify` exists but is not in `npm run verify` or CI, and `README.md:239-241`
  says the screenshots are not drift-checked while 222–223 and 485 say they are
- `run --headless --console video --screenshot <file>` now does in one process what
  `capture-screens.mjs` does with a server and `dbg screen png` — optional, but
  simpler for the shots that need no typing

## 6. Should — pages

Depends on §0. Every page below describes the TMS9918A as the emulator's card:

- **`docs/assembly/video.md`** — the intro (3–6), "Sixteen colors" (53–56), "Talking
  to the card directly" (119–161: two addresses, "eight mode registers", the
  `sei`/`cli` warning at 150–157, which port B makes unnecessary on the new card)
- **`docs/assembly/graphics.md`** — the mode table (11–15) with Graphics II and
  Multicolor, "256 × 192" (8), "32 sprites … one color" (17–18), "16 KB is the
  ceiling" (146–151), the `sei`/`cli` warning (35–41), the Graphics II (77–103) and
  Multicolor (105–131) sections, and "the emulator is a faithful 9918A" (161)
- **`docs/f18a/`**, all six pages, and its nav in `docs/.vitepress/config.mts:263-273`
  — "The emulator is a faithful TMS9918A … eight registers" (`index.md:17-25`), the
  3-bit register decode (`unlocking.md:29-31, 42-44`, `registers.md:24-37`), four
  sprites a line (`sprites.md:27-29`) and the Y + 1 convention (`sprites.md:96-99`),
  which this card no longer follows
- **`docs/using/sound-and-video.md`** 3, 56–65; **`docs/basic/sound-and-video.md`** 3,
  14–15, 21–34, 96–100; **`docs/index.md:55`**; **`docs/the-ace.md:32-36`** —

## 8. Could — new material

Only under decision 1, and only once the samples above are settled:

- A chapter for the new modes. The emulator repository has two cartridges written
  for this card, `samples/vdp-modes/` (the four geometries at 1, 2, 4 and 8 bpp) and
  `samples/vdp-layers/` (two scrolling layers, sprite priority), each with a README
  walking through what it sets and why
- Detection (§16) on `docs/assembly/detection.md`, beside the slot table
- The port-B convention for interrupt handlers (§4, §17), replacing the `sei`/`cli`
  warnings
- Whatever Kernal entry points [6502-BIOS.md](6502-BIOS.md) §5 turns into
